### What changes in the spec because of this

- Cboe delayed JSON becomes the VIX-complex source (VIX1D / VIX9D / VIX3M),
  labelled `~15-min delayed`. Today's read (VIX1D 10.6 under VIX 16.0) is the
  "no event priced into today" shape — a regime input, not a tick.
- ESUSD overnight bars become the overnight-range source, labelled delayed.
  NQ has no source on this plan.
- The `news`, `option_trades:TICKER`, `gex:TICKER` and `price:TICKER` socket
  channels are verified for desk use; wiring them into the scheduled frame is
  a tooling task (extend `options-expert/tools/uw_stream.py`, which already
  handles the drop-oldest queue).
- Level 2 is usable only through a top-n extractor.
- The scoring DB stays unprobed until the token is present in the runtime that
  runs the desk.
