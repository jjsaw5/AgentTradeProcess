1. **It is a median of one observation.** A single contract is not a median, and

### What the run established that nothing predicted

1. **Displayed spread and displayed size are close to independent, and the spec
   only measures spread.** Fifteen samples, both DTE regimes, both option types,
   both sides of the book. The tightest quote in a sample repeatedly had a
   one-lot side. Today's endpoint: a 126,780-volume contract with **no bid** and
   1,048 offered.
2. **Percentage spread is not a liquidity measure below roughly $0.20 of
   premium.** The chain's tick is $0.01 below a $3.00 cutoff, so a $0.065 mark is
   mechanically ≥15% and a $0.015 mark is 66%. Observed on contracts with
   94,022, 108,975 and 124,515 contracts of volume.
3. **The session spread profile is a function of time-to-expiry, not time of
   day.** 0DTE widens monotonically into the force-close (1.908→2.893→4.174;
   1.438→2.667→3.338; 1.243→4.380→7.229). DTE=1 dips into the doldrums and
   recovers partway (2.273→1.036→1.489; 1.961→1.105→1.426). Five sessions, no
   crossovers.
4. **Two of the six predictions cannot be sampled on Tuesdays or Thursdays**,
   because TSLA has no 0DTE contract then, and the schedule runs weekdays.
5. **`gex-levels`' `oi` source emits implausible values on expiry days** —
   put_wall 105–135 on a $346 underlying, at 5 of 6 expiry-day samples and 0 of 3
