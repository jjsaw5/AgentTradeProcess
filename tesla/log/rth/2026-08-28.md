inside P1's declared 10:00–15:00 window**, on a 0DTE session — the exact
population and window P1 specifies. On its own terms, **P1's near-money half is
falsified by this sample.**

Three things must be said alongside that, and none of them is a reason to discard
it:

1. **It is a median of one observation.** A single contract is not a median, and
   this log has said so before (2026-08-26 Sample B, also n=1 after a large move).
2. **The result is robust to the band, for what that is worth.** Widening the
   near-money definition to 0.25–0.65 delta admits the 345 put (6.316%) and the
   347.50 put (3.871%); the median of {3.871, 4.380, 6.316} is **4.380%** —
   identical. Narrowing it changes nothing, since only one contract qualifies.
   So the crossing is not an artifact of where the delta boundary sits.
3. **One in-window sample per session is thin evidence for a threshold.** Across
   five sessions the in-window (Sample B) 0DTE readings are **2.893%** (08-24),
   **2.667%** (08-26, n=1) and **4.380%** (08-28, n=1). One of three exceeds 4%.

**Recorded as: P1's stated falsification condition is met by this sample.**
Whether one in-window observation on one contract should overturn the prediction
is a judgement for the stopping-rule summary, not for this log entry, and **P1 is
not revised here** (§9).
